Our story

Cooked over fire, the way our grandmothers did it.

Tandoori & Co. started as a single copper oven and a family recipe book. Every naan is still hand-stretched to order, every kebab still finished a few inches from real embers — no shortcuts, no microwaves hiding in the back.

The dining room borrows its glow from old Mumbai's Irani cafés and the lantern-lit souks further west: brass, candlelight, and enough noise to feel like a celebration, not a museum.

  • Kitchen: Wood-fired tandoor, open to view
  • Seating: 70 covers indoors, 20 on the terrace
  • Best for: Date nights, family dinners, private events
