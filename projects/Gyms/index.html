Since 2015

Not just a gym — a training ground built around your goals.

Power Track opened in 2015 with one floor and a handful of members. Eleven years on, we're a full-service fitness studio across 8,000 sq. ft — weights, cardio, functional training, and dedicated studios for yoga and group classes.

Our philosophy is simple: sustainable results come from proper form, consistent coaching, and a plan that fits your actual life — not a one-size-fits-all routine.
