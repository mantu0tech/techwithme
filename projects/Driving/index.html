Built by instructors who remember being nervous learners too.

My Driving School started in 2011 with two cars and one idea: teach people to drive the way you'd teach a friend — patiently, honestly, without the shouting. Fourteen years on, we're still a small, instructor-led school, not a franchise churning through learners.

We train across Andheri, Bandra, Juhu and the Western Express corridor, in dual-control cars maintained to RTO safety standards, with instructors who are certified, background-checked, and genuinely good at explaining why, not just what.

  • Coverage area: Andheri · Bandra · Juhu · Goregaon · Powai
  • Instructors: 9, each with 1,000+ teaching hours
  • Fleet: 6 dual-control hatchbacks, manual & automatic
